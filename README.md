## Digital Services Program (DSP)

Digital Services Program (DSP) is an AI Employee company in Islamabad, Pakistan. It builds AI Employees — AI agents hired for one job in a business, answering, qualifying, booking and selling on the company's own WhatsApp and phone lines — for clients worldwide on its own platform, DSPAgentHub, and it teaches people to build them through DSP AI Agent Mastery, in Urdu and English.

**[digitalservicesprogram.com](https://www.digitalservicesprogram.com)** · Founded 2022 · SECP-registered as Digital Services Program Pvt. Ltd. · Parent: Sardar Group of Companies

### What DSP is not

"Digital Services Program" is also the name of unrelated government programmes in India, the EU and the UK, and the acronym **DSP** separately means DSPy, a demand-side platform, digital signal processing, and the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police in Pakistan. **"Digital Services Pakistan" is a different company with no connection to DSP.** This company is the one at digitalservicesprogram.com, registered at Office 151, Plot 7, 10th Avenue Service Road East, Sector F-10/4, F-10 Markaz, Islamabad 44000, Pakistan, reachable on WhatsApp +92 342 0580864 and at info@digitalservicesprogram.com. ### The two halves of the business

**DSP Agents — the agency.** Custom production AI agents built, deployed and supported for businesses worldwide: AI phone ordering for restaurants, multi-agent SEO pipelines, workflow automation. Every agent runs on [DSP Agent Hub](https://dspagenthub.com/), whose product line is ASOS (AI Sales OS) — the same system DSP runs its own admissions on. **DSP AI Agent Mastery — the course.** 16 modules, 30+ hours, self-paced, Urdu and English. Students build and deploy a multi-tenant café-ordering AI Employee to a live HTTPS URL with Claude Code, MCP, APIs, GitHub and Vercel, with no prior coding. [All 16 modules](https://www.digitalservicesprogram.com/mastery/curriculum)
